Professional Journey

From Biotechnology to Agentic AI

My story is one of radical transformation driven by a singular obsession: understanding deep patterns. It began in the precision-intensive halls of biology at SCTIMST, where I spent years studying molecular dynamics and designing rigorous scientific experiments. In that world, 'data' wasn't just numbers—it was the lifeblood of discovery, requiring a level of discipline and analytical rigor that would later become my greatest asset in engineering.

In 2023, I made the calculated pivot from biological systems to digital intelligence. I realized that the same scientific mindset used to decode cellular behavior could be applied to architecting artificial neurons. I deep-dived into the world of Data Science, rapidly advancing from classical machine learning to the cutting edge of Large Language Models and Agentic architectures.

Today, as a Gen AI Engineer at Blackbox Learning, I occupy the intersection of these two worlds. I build intelligent RAG pipelines and multimodal agents with the precision of a researcher and the agility of a software engineer. My mission isn't just to build 'smart' apps, but to design agentic systems that exhibit genuine reasoning and utility.

Beyond the code, I am a mentor at heart. Having guided over 100+ students, I believe that the future of AI belongs to those who pair technical mastery with a deep understanding of the human and scientific context. I don't just teach models; I teach the philosophy of building responsible, high-performance intelligence.
