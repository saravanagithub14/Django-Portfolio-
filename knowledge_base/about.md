Saravana Perumal R is a biologist turned AI engineer. Since April 2026 Saravana has worked as a Gen AI Engineer at Blackbox Learning in Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum), Kerala, India, with a focus on generative AI and agentic AI.

Saravana started in the life sciences. Saravana holds a BSc in Botany and Biotechnology from the University of Kerala (2013 to 2016) and an MSc in Biotechnology and Bioinformatics from Kerala University of Fisheries & Ocean Studies (2016 to 2018), and qualified UGC-NET for Assistant Professor in Life Sciences in 2019. Research roles followed at IISER Thiruvananthapuram (stem cell biology), ICAR's Central Institute of Brackishwater Aquaculture in Chennai (a vaccine against Nervous Necrosis Virus in sea bass), and Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology, where Saravana spent over four years as a Senior Research Fellow in biomedical technology working on 3D bioprinted skin, hydrogels, and nanomaterials. That research produced two book chapters and two research articles.

In 2023 Saravana moved into data science through a Diploma in Data Science and an internship at Edure, then spent just over two years as a Jr. Data Scientist and Data Scientist at Dexpro Innovations Pvt Ltd. Those roles mixed data analysis and statistical modeling with building training material and mentoring students into data science jobs.

Alongside that work, Saravana has built 18 personal AI and machine learning projects, including RAG chatbots on Groq, Pinecone, ChromaDB and AstraDB, LangChain agents, LLM fine-tuning with LoRA and QLoRA, generative AI apps on AWS Bedrock and Lambda, and deep learning models for medical imaging and computer vision. Saravana's personal motto is "Learn and Transform."
